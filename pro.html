Why this exists

In 1983, Richard Dennis bet his partner William Eckhardt that trading discipline could be taught to complete novices. He was right — the "Turtles" traded a fixed, written rule set for years and made money, not because the rule was magic, but because they didn't override it. Most retail investors do the opposite: they have a plan and abandon it exactly when it matters most. This simulation puts a number on that specific habit, round by round, instead of just telling you it's a problem.

Not financial advice. Illustrative tools for training a decision habit, not investment recommendations.
