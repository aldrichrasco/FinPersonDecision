Real money games

The same games economists actually use to study trust and cooperation, with real money on the line. Play one, then see how your instinct compares to what pure rationality predicts — and to what real people actually do.

How this works

1. Pick a game and a role. Each one pits textbook rational self-interest against how people actually behave.

2. Play it out. Your "opponent" is a simulated classmate — pick which archetype, and their moves are shaped by that archetype's real risk and prosocial-orientation scores.

3. Compare. See the equilibrium prediction next to the real research finding, plus how everyone else who's played has actually done.

Share a code with your class so "what everyone else has done" only counts your students.
